SAC picking · Wadati · three-circle radical centre · joint least-squares hypocenter (homogeneous half-space)

Three-circle method (N ≥ 3 stations): circles of radius Dhyp = Δt·Vp/(Vp/Vs − 1); the epicentre is the radical centre (least-squares intersection of all pairwise radical axes), which is independent of the assumed depth. Vp must be supplied (input value or joint-LS estimate): S−P times from three stations cannot resolve it. Three spheres: the same S−P distances as spheres, solved for (x, y, h) with depth as an explicit unknown. Joint LS (N ≥ 4): weighted Levenberg–Marquardt for (x, y, h, To[, Vp][, Vp/Vs]) with a depth scan, optional Huber weights and station elevations. Uncertainty: Monte Carlo (Wadati refitted in every trial), asymptotic covariance, and in-browser Bayesian sampling.

7) Joint least-squares inversion

Trusted stations used in the inversion: -
Only the ticked stations enter the joint inversion, its Monte Carlo, the Bayesian sampling, the velocity sensitivity and the joint inversions of the case study (section 9). Stations not ticked “Use” in section 3, or without picks, are greyed out. P/S shows which picks a station has.
Vp Vp/Vs
Station clocks
Uses the stations selected above, including stations with only a P or only an S pick (≥ 4 stations with Vp estimated). The depth is found by scanning the misfit from the surface to 40 km and then refining freely; the scan is plotted next to the map. Station clocks: each station with P and S is tested for a common offset of both arrivals (a clock or timing error, F test at the 1% family-wise level); a significant offset is estimated as a station term, so that the station contributes only its S−P time, and Vp/Vs is refitted by the Wadati regression without it. Weak priors: Vp ~ N(input Vp, 0.5²) and Vp/Vs ~ N(Wadati, 0.1²) when estimated (the priors of section 8), which stabilize the depth–Vp trade-off of sparse networks. Depth resolution compares the nearest station distance with the depth, reports the depth-scan interval and checks the depth against an S−P-only (clock-free) sphere fit.

8) Bayesian sampling (in the browser)

iterations per chain
Posterior: -
Adaptive Metropolis, two chains started at the least-squares solution; Vp and Vp/Vs are sampled with weakly informative priors (Vp ~ N(input, 0.5²), Vp/Vs ~ N(Wadati, 0.1²), depth uniform from the surface to 50 km). Converged when R̂ < 1.05. The posterior depth histogram is overlaid on the depth scan.

9) Case study: all solutions and figures

Runs the Wadati fit, the joint inversion with the settings of section 7 and three variants (Vp and Vp/Vs estimated; Vp fixed; Huber weights; depth fixed at the SAC-header depth), the comparison with and without the stations flagged by the leave-one-out screen (with a clock-error test: a station whose P and S residuals share one offset while its S−P time fits is reported as a probable timing error), the three-circle and sphere solutions (stations ticked in section 6, or the best-distributed three), Monte Carlo, Bayesian sampling and the velocity sensitivity, and draws the figures of the case study. Each figure can be saved as SVG or 300-dpi PNG; the ZIP also contains the solutions table (CSV and Markdown), the figure captions and the session.
