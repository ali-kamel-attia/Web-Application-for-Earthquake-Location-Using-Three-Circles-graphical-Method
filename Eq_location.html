Three-circle bisection + N-station joint LS hypocenter inversion

Chord method (3 stations): Vp derived as apparent velocity from Wadati To and chord geometry, iterated to the fixed point Vp = median(Dhyp_geom_i / (Tp_i − To)). Epicenter = LS intersection of 3 chord lines; depth from h = √(Dhyp² − Depi²). Uncertainties via Monte Carlo over σ(pick), σ(Vp), σ(Vp/Vs).
Joint LS (N ≥ 3 stations): Levenberg–Marquardt on the full arrival-time likelihood with unknowns (x, y, h, To, Vp) and Vp/Vs fixed from Wadati. Uncertainties from the asymptotic covariance σ²·(JᵀJ)⁻¹; depth becomes well-resolved once N ≥ 5.
